# Scope and operating safety

## Design boundaries

- The home plasma source is the intact, commercially enclosed Vernier ST-SPS with its matched sealed spectrum tubes. Do not modify, open, defeat the interlock, or attach a homemade electrical driver.
- Never use the optional degassing chamber for an electrical discharge, and never bring wiring through its lid.
- No compressed deuterium, homemade gas production, neutron experiments, or attempt to infer fusion from visible light.
- If progressing to an IEC demonstration chamber, first obtain a sponsoring lab's equipment and radiation review, including electrical engineering and vacuum expertise. U of T's X-ray safety office determines applicability of its process for the proposed apparatus.

## Each home capture session

1. Check the supply, cord, tube carrier and workspace for damage; stop if damaged.
2. Keep liquids, loose metal, exposed wiring and the optical mount clear of the tube and supply. The optical instrument is passive and electrically separate.
3. Insert only the manufacturer's matched tube. Operate according to its manual and do not bypass the interlock.
4. Record start/stop times. Treat the tube as hot after use and allow it to cool before handling, as directed by the manual.
5. Shut off/unplug as directed when finished; store tubes protected from breakage.

For the optional vacuum module, inspect the vessel and lid, use manufacturer-rated parts, guard against implosion, and vent according to the manufacturer's procedure. Do not use a household jar. University vacuum guidance treats all evacuated glass as an implosion hazard: https://ehrs.upenn.edu/health-safety/lab-safety/chemical-hygiene-plan/standard-operating-procedures/sop-pressure-and-0
