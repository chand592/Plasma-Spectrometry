| Logging upgrade, if gauge is inadequate | optional | Quote first | Select a sensor only after confirming pressure range, compatibility, and accuracy. |

The optional kit is intended for pump-down and leak-rate studies. A resin degassing chamber is not a fusor chamber. Do not buy it expecting to turn it into one.

### Procurement decision

Do not order the expensive supply until the optical prototype has produced a measurable spectrum from a harmless light source, the available workspace can accommodate the manufacturer's clearance and electrical requirements, and you have read its current manual. Ask a lab if it can loan a compatible supply and tubes before purchasing your own.
